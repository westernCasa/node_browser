Principle

Shell for behaviour

NODE treats research as a behaviour and builds the browser around it.

Features

What it does

The next search, already typed.

Suggestions come from the page you are reading, not from an ad auction.

Four streams. One screen.

Up to 4 tabs play at once in Mosaic.

Every source. In order. Exportable.

Every page you read in a session is kept with its keywords and saved as Markdown.

Share a trail. Merge it back.

Send a trail as one Markdown file, talk it through in threads, and merge the trails you receive without losing anyone's notes.

Ask the page. Local or cloud.

Ask AI works with a local model or Grok, and page text is sent only when you ask.

  • Threads

    Start a thread for each open question in a trail, reply, and mark it resolved with a conclusion.

  • Research agent

    Ask a question and get a short report with numbered sources.

  • Talk

    See the Hacker News and Bluesky posts that link to the page you are reading.

  • Site rules

    Set autoplay, sound, Mosaic and blocking for each site.

  • Passwords

    Save and fill logins, encrypted by your computer's own secure storage.

  • Agents

    Claude Code, Codex and Cursor can search and read through NODE, read-only.

Privacy

What NODE sends

Stays here
History, trails, passwords and the local model stay on your computer.
Sent when you ask
Page text goes to Grok only when you ask it something.
Blocked
Ads and trackers are blocked with uBlock Origin's default lists.
Never read
Sign-in, banking and payment pages are never read or sent.
