Building NODE

I'm Sven, and I make NODE. These are the questions I run into while building it, what I decide and why. Newest first. Anything marked open or proposed can still change. RSS feed.

18 Nov 2026Open

How do I know if people come back, without tracking them?

Thinking
I haven't decided yet. What I'm weighing is adding a "days since install" bucket (0, 1, 2 to 7, 8 to 30, 31 and up) to the anonymous daily count.
Why
The daily count has no id, so it can't tell a new user from an old one. A bucket with no id would show me how many people stick around without following anyone.
Next
Until I decide, a 5-person usability test is the only way I'll know how the first week goes.
18 Nov 2026Proposed

What should the first five minutes of NODE feel like?

Plan
I want first launch to ask "What are you looking into?" instead of asking for API keys. Every way in should end with a trail of 3 cards, the canvas open, and an offer to send it.
Why
Right now the first screen is a setup form. The trail, the part that makes NODE different, shows up later on a screen I never point anyone to.
Next
I'm aiming to build it for 0.13 and test it with 5 people. It passes if 4 of 5 get to 3 cards and the canvas on their own in under 5 minutes.
18 Nov 2026Proposed

How far do I go to make NODE a habit?

Plan
No feed, no streaks and no fake urgency. Every kind of notice gets its own switch, and first-week milestones are counted only on your computer. If a change brings more visits but also more people turning notices off, I roll it back.
Why
A lot of you picked NODE for privacy. And the long experiments I read, at Facebook and Pinterest, found that pushing harder loses people over a few months.
18 Nov 2026Decided

Should syncing between devices need an account?

Decided
No. You pair devices with a code or a QR code, and your trails are encrypted on your device before they're sent. The server deletes each change once your other devices have it, after 30 days at most. This shipped in 0.12.0.
Why
An account would be the first thing NODE knows about you. A pairing code is enough to prove two devices belong together.

15 Nov 2026Decided

Should NODE count how many people use it?

Decided
Yes, with one anonymous count a day: the version and your operating system, with no id. You can turn it off in Settings → Help. This shipped in 0.11.0.
Why
Download numbers didn't tell me whether anyone kept using NODE. Nothing about what you read or search is sent.

6 Nov 2026Decided

How many notifications should NODE send?

Decided
Few, and only about something new. There's a weekly limit across all kinds, a switch for each kind, and I'll only ask for system permission on day 3 or later. I judge them by how many people turn them off, not by clicks.
Why
The long experiments I read found that fewer, more relevant notifications raise use over time. More of them raise visits for a while, then more people opt out.
5 Nov 2026Decided

Who is NODE for?

Decided
Knowledge networking between friends and small groups. You map what you read, and send the whole trail to a friend or a group as one file.
Why
Research usually gets passed on as a list of links, and the next person starts over. NODE already records the path, so sending it takes no copying.
5 Nov 2026Decided

What should NODE be called?

Decided
NODE Research Browser. I dropped Scout and NODE R-X.
Why
"Research Browser" says what it is. "Node Browser" on its own collides with Node.js, and an X in the name reads as a tie to xAI.
5 Nov 2026Decided

Do I share trails as links or as files?

Decided
As one plain Markdown file first. Links come once the share server works for everyone.
Why
A file works today, anyone can read it in a text editor, and it outlives the app.
3 Nov 2026Parked

Should two people's trails merge into one?

For now
Not yet. I'm focusing on one person building a trail and sharing it. When merging comes back, it starts with showing both trails on one canvas before combining anything.
Why
I couldn't find a study on whether people understand two merged research maps. Git-style merging makes sense to developers, but most people researching together don't think that way.
3 Nov 2026Decided

Is a trail a timeline or a map?

Decided
A canvas, with the timeline kept beside it and Replay for whoever receives it. NODE draws the links it already knows. Drawing your own is optional.
Why
In the studies I read, people rarely draw links by hand, and a bare map has no clear place to start. A map plus a replay of how it was built got 71% of the facts across at handoff, against 50%.
25 Oct 2026Decided

Can NODE run Chrome extensions?

Decided
No. Instead, NODE's built-in blocker uses the same filter lists uBlock Origin uses by default.
Why
A beta tester asked for uBlock Origin. Electron loads extensions but is missing the parts ad blockers need, and the one way that works clashes with NODE's own blocker.
18 Oct 2026In progress

Electron or a Chromium build?

So far
Electron stays the release build. I've started a Chromium build on ungoogled-chromium, mainly so Google sign-in works inside NODE.
Why
Google blocks sign-in inside Electron apps, and faking Chrome breaks Google's terms. Mosaic and the address-bar suggestions need changes to Chromium itself, so I'll only switch once it does everything the current build does.
