NODE motion

01 · Sound toggle

Sound: one / all

Border and text fade to violet when it turns on (200ms, ease-enter) and back when it turns off (120ms, ease-exit). The word swaps instantly. The colour follows the state NODE confirms, so the demo waits 40ms like the app does.

02 · Card "read" state

A suggestion you opened

The card dims to 60% (200ms, ease-move) and a "read" badge fades in (200ms, ease-enter). It only goes one way: a card never turns back to unread.

03 · Mosaic button

Mosaic / Focus

The new label rises 3px and fades in (200ms, ease-enter); the old one goes at once. The colour change is the same as 01. (The tiles themselves are 08.)

04 · Bookmark star

Save and remove a bookmark

Save: the filled star scales from 0.6 to 1 and fades up from 0.4 (200ms, ease-enter). Remove: the outline star fades up from 0.4 with no scale (120ms, ease-exit). Switching tabs repaints the star with no animation; "Switch tab" shows that.

05 · Research agent steps

Each new step types out

Only new lines animate: each one is revealed left to right in 14 even steps over 160ms. Lines already listed stay still, and the list keeps scrolling to the newest line.

    06 · Keyword chips

    Pin, unpin and hide keywords

    Click a chip to pin it: it turns violet (200ms, ease-enter) and every chip slides to its new place (200ms, ease-move). Click × to hide one: it fades and shrinks to 0.9 in place (120ms, ease-exit), the others slide over, and the next keyword scales in from 0.95 (200ms, ease-enter). A fast second click waits for the first.

    07 · Result cards

    Cards arrive and leave

    New cards fade in (320ms, ease-enter), each starting 40ms after the one before; cards already on screen stay still. × fades a card out (120ms, ease-exit), then the gap closes (320ms, ease-move) before the card is removed.

    08 · Mosaic tab windows

    Tabs move into and out of the grid

    Focus → Mosaic: the other tabs wait in their tiles underneath; the active tab shrinks into its tile (320ms, ease-out). Mosaic → Focus: the active tile grows over the others (200ms, ease-out), and they are hidden once covered. Opening or closing a tab in Mosaic moves every tile to its new cell (200ms, ease-in-out); a new tab appears in place.

    The real tab windows use Electron's built-in curves: ease-out stands in for --ease-enter and ease-in-out for --ease-move, so this page uses the same CSS keywords.

    09 · Full-screen toolbar

    The toolbar slides up and comes back down

    In full screen the toolbar leaves at the top and the page moves up with it (200ms, ease-exit: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)). At the top edge it comes back down and pushes the page down (320ms, ease-enter: cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.2, 1)). Reduced motion: no movement; the toolbar fades in.
