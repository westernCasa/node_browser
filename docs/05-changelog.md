## Testing notes

- Testing ran in Electron 32.3.3 until 2026-10-13 and in Electron 44.7.0 (Chromium 152) since, under a virtual display, against local test pages (an article with navigation, footer, image, a link that opens a new tab, an automatic popup, and a page that asks for location and notifications).
- The test sandbox could not reach Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo or Brave, so their responses were stubbed. This checked request building, fallback and the UI, but not live result quality.
- Checked: keyword chips, queries, pins and hides, text selection searches, popup blocking and allowed popups, muted background tabs, S shortcut, permission prompts, DuckDuckGo blocking and fallback, Brave key rejection and success, lazy previews, a hidden keyword leaving the query, Wikipedia-first timing (about 1.3 s with a 3 s web source), site rules (extract skip/include, popups block, autoplay off, suggestions off, live reload, broken file), find, zoom, history, research trail, dismiss, read badges, export, and pins after restart.
- B20 checks (fake local and Grok servers): Test and model list, bad key, wrong model name, AI queries in search, what is sent to the model (no page text or address), local model down.
- After the shared-core refactor, all 6 end-to-end suites were rerun and their results compared with the previous runs (same keywords, queries, timings within 0.1 s, same session and history behaviour).
- Mobile: bundles for iOS and Android compile (Metro and Hermes); the shared controller is tested in Node. The app has not been run on a device or simulator yet.
- B32-B34: all 8 end-to-end suites pass; suites 01-06 give the same results as before (two tests changed how they count tabs, since hidden tabs are now detached from the window). Page-reading output compared old against new on three pages at four scroll positions: identical. Measured before and after with `07-performance`. Mobile bundles still compile.
- The tests are in the repo under `test/e2e`; see its README to rerun them.
