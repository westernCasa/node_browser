    Privacy

    Ads and trackers

    Uses the EasyList and EasyPrivacy lists, updated weekly. To allow ads on one site, add a site rule with "adblock": false.

    AI agents

    Agent access (MCP)

    Agents such as Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can search with your sources, read public pages in a separate logged-out session, read tabs you share (Share tab in the toolbar) and read your research sessions. Read-only: they cannot click, type or sign in. Sign-in, payment and banking pages are never read. Only programs on this computer with the token can connect.

    Accounts

    Google window

    Google does not allow signing in inside NODE's engine. These pages open in a separate window of a Chromium-based browser on your computer (Brave, Chromium, Edge, Vivaldi or Chrome), with its own NODE profile. Google Search, YouTube and Scholar searches stay in NODE.
