    Search

    AI query ideas

    An AI model suggests extra search queries from your pinned keywords, the page's top keywords and its title. It never receives the page text or address.

    Privacy

    Ads and trackers

    Uses the filter lists uBlock Origin turns on by default (uBlock filters, EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Peter Lowe's list and others), updated weekly. Blocks ad and tracker requests and hides leftover ad boxes on the page. Hiding cookie notices adds the cookie-notice lists; a hidden notice is not an answer to it. To allow ads on one site, add a site rule with "adblock": false.

    Startup

    Tabs

    To choose which tabs Mosaic shows, press ⌘⇧\ (Ctrl+Shift+\ on Windows) or right-click the Mosaic button, and click the tabs you want. You can also right-click a tab and choose Show in Mosaic. With none chosen, Mosaic shows your first tabs and the one you are on.

    Tabs you have not opened for 15 minutes go to sleep to save memory; they load again when you click them. Tabs playing media never sleep.

    AI agents

    Agent access (MCP)

    Claude app (desktop): it starts its tools as programs, so NODE adds a small bridge program to the Claude app's settings file. Restart the Claude app afterwards. NODE has to be open with Agents on while you use it.

    Agents such as Claude Code, Codex or Cursor can search with your sources, read public pages in a separate logged-out session, read tabs you share (Share tab in the toolbar) and read your research sessions. Read-only: they cannot click, type or sign in. Sign-in, payment and banking pages are never read. Only programs on this computer with the token can connect.

    Accounts

    Google window

    Google does not allow signing in inside NODE's engine. These pages open in a separate window of a Chromium-based browser on your computer (Brave, Chromium, Edge, Vivaldi or Chrome), with its own NODE profile. Google Search, YouTube and Scholar searches stay in NODE.

    Help

    Problems

    Copy diagnostics puts NODE's version, your system, which features are on and recent errors on the clipboard, without page text, addresses you typed or keys. Paste it into a bug report.

    Beta survey: about 4 minutes, anonymous. Copy usage summary puts counts on the clipboard to paste into the survey: days used, suggestions shown and opened, pins, hides, trail pages and a few others. No pages, addresses or keywords, and nothing is sent by NODE.

    Gestures and shortcuts: pinch on the trackpad to move between one tab, Mosaic and all your tabs. The tutorial takes about 2 minutes, on practice tabs.

    On a Mac, passkeys need a signed NODE that Apple has approved as a browser. Until then they are off, so sites such as X offer password sign-in instead of waiting for a passkey. Takes effect after restarting NODE.
