## Description (4,000)

NODE is a web browser for research. It keeps a trail of what you read so you can find your way back, see how sources connect, and share what you found.

TRAILS
• NODE opens on your trail: a timeline of the pages you read, newest first.
• Pages you spend time on become cards on a canvas. Move them, link them (supports, contradicts, answers, raises), group them, and add notes and questions.
• Start a new trail for each topic and switch between them with a swipe.

CAPTURE
• Select text on any page and tap Capture. NODE saves the quote, the page, the time and a thumbnail to your trail.
• If the page has a DOI or PubMed ID, NODE saves that too.

FULL TEXT FOR PAPERS
• On an article with a DOI, tap Full text to find a free, legal copy through Unpaywall and OpenAlex, or open it through your library.

MOSAIC
• See up to 4 pages at once. Choose Auto, Stack, Side by side, Grid or Focus.
• Videos play muted in the background; only the page you're on has sound.

SHARE
• Send a trail as a small text file. The person you send it to can open it in NODE and replay how you got there.

ASK AI (OPTIONAL)
• Ask questions about the page you're reading. Use the built-in demo, your own Grok key, or your own model server.
• NODE asks before sending any page text to an AI service.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics. Your trails stay on your phone.
